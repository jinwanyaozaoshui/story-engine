# Design Principles

1. **Consequences must persist.** A stage result should alter at least one meaningful state: identity, permissions, resources, responsibilities, relationships, knowledge, public labels, or medium/scene state.
2. **A stage goal is not a lifelong desire.** It should be settleable, fail-able, and specific to the current stage.
3. **Choices need trade-offs.** Multiple options are only meaningful when benefits and costs differ.
4. **Risk rules are diagnostic, not verdicts.** Repetition, resets, or temporary imbalance can be intentional and productive.
5. **Do not invent canon.** New story content must be marked as suggestion, inference, or hypothesis unless confirmed by the user.
6. **Evaluation claims must be reproducible.** Deterministic repository checks and model/Agent behavior evaluations are reported separately.

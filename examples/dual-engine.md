# Example: Dual Engine

## Input

> By day, the protagonist runs a small shop and must pay rent and retain employees. At night, the protagonist investigates anomalous incidents. A special object recovered at night improves shop security but also attracts a regulatory inspection. Employees begin to suspect that the protagonist is hiding something.

## Diagnosis

Rules: `SE-RC-004`, `SE-F-003`.

| Engine | Goal / pressure | Result feedback | Persistent consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reality | Rent, employees, shop safety | Security improves but oversight escalates | Employee suspicion, regulatory pressure |
| Hidden | Investigate anomalous incidents | Obtain a special object | The result flows back into real-world operations and relationships |

Both lines currently perform real functions. The hidden result is not merely a reward; it changes constraints and relationships on the reality side. The supplied material is insufficient to judge long-term imbalance.
